ABOUT ROOTFROND

Smart NPK Soil Sensing + Leaf Image Analysis

Understand Your Coconut Palms with Soil + Leaf Intelligence

RootFrond is a proposed portable soil-and-leaf diagnostic system for Sri Lankan coconut growers. NPK sensors estimate nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium levels, while AI-assisted leaf and tree image analysis helps cross-check the sensor readings. The goal is clearer nutrient guidance and less fertilizer guesswork.

FARMER CHALLENGES

Real challenges. Smarter solutions.

From limited laboratory access to difficult nutrient decisions, coconut growers face challenges that affect everyday farming. RootFrond is designed to bring useful soil and tree-health information closer to the farmer.

01 / 06

  • Laboratory Accessibility 01 / 06
    ACCESS TO SOIL TESTING

    Accessing soil-testing services can involve transporting samples to distant laboratories and waiting for results. This makes regular testing difficult for many farmers.

    RootFrond approach: Portable soil monitoring aims to provide convenient field information between professional laboratory tests.

    DOA
    Department of Agriculture Soil-testing guidance ↗
  • Testing Restrictions 02 / 06
    CRI LEAF-SAMPLING CONDITIONS

    CRI's Differential Fertilizer Recommendation (DFR) service requires specific sampling conditions. Leaf samples cannot be collected during rainy seasons or within six months after fertilizer application.

    RootFrond approach: Soil observations and guided frond photography can help farmers monitor conditions between formal testing periods.

    CRI
    Coconut Research Institute Fertilizer advisory A5 ↗
  • Fertilizer Management 03 / 06
    DIFFERENT SOILS, DIFFERENT NEEDS

    Soil conditions and nutrient needs can differ between growing areas. Applying the same fertilizer mixture without considering these differences can lead to unnecessary fertilizer use.

    RootFrond approach: Farm-specific soil records and location-aware information aim to support better-informed fertilizer decisions.

    CRI
    Coconut Research Institute Fertilizer recommendations ↗
  • Coconut Tree Health 04 / 06
    CONFUSING LEAF SYMPTOMS

    Yellow coconut fronds do not always indicate one nutrient deficiency. Drought, waterlogging and other environmental conditions can cause similar visible symptoms.

    RootFrond approach: AI-assisted coconut frond analysis aims to examine visible symptoms together with soil information, while identifying uncertain results.

    CRI
    Coconut Research Institute Nutrient deficiency advisory ↗
  • Nutrient Deficiencies 05 / 06
    NOT ALL PROBLEMS ARE VISIBLE

    Coconut palms can experience hidden mineral deficiencies without showing obvious leaf symptoms. Chemical leaf analysis is needed to confirm these hidden nutrient problems.

    RootFrond approach: Combining different observations may help identify possible risks, while directing farmers to laboratory analysis when confirmation is required.

    CRI
    Coconut Research Institute Hidden nutrient deficiencies ↗
  • Sustainable Farming 06 / 06
    AVOIDING FERTILIZER WASTE

    Applying more fertilizer than a palm needs can waste money and resources. CRI's Differential Fertilizer Recommendation method is designed to support more suitable fertilizer mixtures based on nutrient assessment.

    RootFrond approach: The system aims to present understandable nutrient guidance and estimated fertilizer costs in LKR, subject to agricultural expert validation.

    CRI
    Coconut Research Institute Differential Fertilizer Guidance ↗

Information is based on agricultural guidance from Sri Lanka's Coconut Research Institute and Department of Agriculture. RootFrond is being developed to support informed farm decisions alongside professional testing and expert advice.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Have Questions? We've Got Answers.

Learn how RootFrond combines NPK soil sensors, leaf-image cross-checks and proposed fertilizer guidance for coconut growers.

🔎︎

What is RootFrond?

RootFrond is a proposed portable soil-and-leaf nutrient assessment system for Sri Lankan coconut growers. It combines NPK sensor estimates with image analysis to support more informed fertilizer choices.

⛯

What exactly do the soil sensors measure?

The planned kit includes NPK sensors to estimate soil nitrogen (N), phosphorus (P) and potassium (K), plus measurements such as pH, electrical conductivity, moisture and temperature. Sensor calibration is important for reliable results.

⸙

Why does RootFrond analyse coconut leaves too?

Photos of coconut fronds or trees will be analysed for patterns associated with nutrient stress. The image findings are intended to cross-check the NPK sensor results, not replace physical testing.

シ

Who is RootFrond designed for?

The first version focuses on Sri Lankan coconut smallholders, estate growers and agricultural field officers who need easier access to field-level nutrient information.

⌘

Will RootFrond work offline and in local languages?

Offline use and Sinhala, Tamil and English guidance are project goals. Bluetooth connectivity, on-device analysis and language support need to be implemented and tested.

⤬

Can RootFrond replace soil laboratories or experts?

No. NPK probes and image models can have measurement uncertainty. RootFrond is planned as decision support, with laboratory verification and qualified agronomic advice recommended before significant fertilizer changes.
